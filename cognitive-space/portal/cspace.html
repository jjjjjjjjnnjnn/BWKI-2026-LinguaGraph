CognitiveSpace 3D

The mathematics subgraph as an explorable 3D knowledge graph — 556 nodes · 525 relations (238 links shown) · 219 trilingual groups.

What is this?

Each bubble is a math concept; each line is a textbook relation (prerequisite, part-of, requires…). Color encodes the education stage. The same graph the paper measures — rendered explorable instead of printed static.

Why explore it in 3D?

The language signal (ΔLDS) is structural: which concepts exist, how they link, how deep chains run. A flat figure shows one projection; the 3D view lets you verify density, hubs and language splits with your own eyes before trusting any single number.
